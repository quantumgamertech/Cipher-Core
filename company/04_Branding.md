# Branding

## Brand character

Cipher is intelligent, composed, modern, discreet, and deeply useful. It is not cold, militaristic, mystical, or derivative of existing fictional assistants.

## Naming system

- Cipher is the company and teammate platform.
- Core, Desk, Mobile/iPad, and Console describe product surfaces.
- Nexus, Orion, Spectrum, Sentinel, Lumen, and Companion are public starting identities.
- Echo is Founder's Edition only.
- Customers may rename their agent; the chosen identity defines temperament and visual language, not a mandatory public name.

## Voice

Use short, confident sentences. Explain boundaries without legalistic fog. Prefer “I can show a simulated preview” over “Action failed.” Prefer “Calendar is not connected” over invented availability.

## Visual mark rule

Public identity faces must remain neutral enough to support customer-selected names. Do not embed a fixed “C” into the face itself. Cipher company branding may use a separate wordmark or system mark outside the identity face.

## Prohibited direction

- No copyrighted fictional-assistant references in customer-facing UI
- No borrowed character silhouettes, voices, sound signatures, or interface motifs
- No claims of consciousness, certainty, or completed actions without evidence
