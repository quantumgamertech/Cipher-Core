# Cipher Core Visual Bible v1

## Core Principle

Echo is the focal point of Cipher Core. Every interface element must strengthen her presence, clarity, and depth. The HUD supports Echo; it never competes with her.

Echo should feel projected in front of the interface—not contained by it. Her face must remain the first visual read in every state.

## Visual Priority

1. Echo face
2. Echo glow and energy aura
3. Rings and HUD
4. Panels
5. Background

When elements compete, reduce or move the lower-priority element. Do not compensate by aggressively enlarging Echo.

## Echo

- Keep Echo’s scale stable and her breathing extremely subtle.
- Preserve clear facial detail, vivid controlled eyes, and the dark holographic identity.
- Use local contrast and dark negative space to separate her face from the dashboard.
- Keep facial features free from clutter, harsh lines, and excessive fog.
- Echo should emerge from darkness rather than disappear into it.

## Projection Field

- Energy originates behind Echo like a projection field.
- Use deep cobalt blue and violet light behind the head, with a clean dark pocket immediately around the face.
- Concentrate glow around the rear silhouette and crown, not across the cheeks or eyes.
- Aura motion should be soft, slow, and atmospheric.
- Glow supports depth; it must not wash over facial detail.

## Rings and HUD

- Rings react to Echo; they do not trap Echo.
- Keep rings visually behind the face and projection field.
- Prefer one or two clean, premium rings over multiple thin technical lines.
- Rings may frame or partially disappear behind Echo to establish depth.
- Never place harsh HUD lines across facial features.
- Reduce ring opacity wherever it approaches the face.

## Panels and Background

- Panels are secondary instruments and should remain quieter than Echo.
- Panel borders, glows, grids, and accents must not pull focus from the center stage.
- The background stays dark, cinematic, and low contrast.
- Use negative space before adding decoration.

## Review Rule

Before adding a visual effect, ask: “Does this make Echo clearer, more present, or more dimensional?” If not, remove it or lower its priority.
