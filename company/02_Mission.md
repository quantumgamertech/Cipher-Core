# Mission

Cipher gives individuals and businesses a trustworthy AI teammate that makes work clearer, more responsive, and easier to operate.

We build for practical outcomes:

- Fewer missed opportunities
- Faster, more consistent customer response
- Clearer operational visibility
- Less repetitive coordination
- Better preparation for human decisions

The mission is not to remove people from their businesses. It is to give them leverage while preserving ownership, judgment, and final authority.

## Operating principles

- Every business deserves a teammate.
- Never pretend. Always explain.
- Ask before crossing a boundary.
- Prefer understandable systems over mysterious automation.
- Design privacy and reversibility into the workflow.
- Treat customer trust as product functionality.
