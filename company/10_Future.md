# Future

## Product sequence

1. Validate identity and onboarding
2. Establish permissions and audit architecture
3. Add local-only providers behind explicit flags
4. Pilot one narrow business role
5. Test reliability, recovery, and customer comprehension
6. Expand through isolated adapters

## Product family future

- Cipher Core remains Kevin's private proving ground.
- Cipher Desk becomes the focused business appliance.
- Cipher Mobile/iPad provides secure companion control.
- Cipher Console becomes the administrative source of truth.

## Guardrails

The future is modular, permission-aware, and evidence-based. Cipher should never become “more alive” by pretending. Real capability must be introduced only after security review, consent design, data-isolation testing, and explicit founder approval.

Echo remains reserved for Founder's Edition Cipher Core. Public identities remain name-flexible and must work without a fixed “C” face.
