# Vision

Cipher becomes a recognizable teammate layer across desk, display, mobile, and business operations. A customer should be able to configure a clear identity, teach it approved business context, and understand exactly what it can do.

## Near horizon

- Prove the visual language, identity system, and onboarding experience locally.
- Establish permission, audit, and adapter contracts before real connections.
- Validate focused roles such as receptionist, scheduler, lead support, and IT helpdesk.

## Long horizon

Cipher Desk becomes a dependable business presence. Cipher Mobile and iPad provide secure oversight. Cipher Console makes setup and control legible. Capabilities expand through isolated, customer-approved modules rather than an opaque universal agent.

Success is not measured by how autonomous Cipher appears. It is measured by how reliably it helps, how clearly it communicates, and how confidently a customer remains in control.
