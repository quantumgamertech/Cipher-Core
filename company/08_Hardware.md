# Hardware

## Cipher Desk

Cipher Desk is a future business AI appliance: a stable, managed presence for front desks, studios, offices, and operations spaces. It should prioritize reliability, privacy visibility, and simple recovery.

## Companion surfaces

- Top monitor: ambient command-center view
- Stream Deck: explicit operator shortcuts
- Thermalright LCD: compact mirror/status surface
- RGB: ambient state only, never the sole status indicator
- iPad/Mobile: authenticated oversight and remote control
- Cipher Console: setup, permissions, health, and administration

## Hardware principles

- No silent discovery or control
- Read and write permissions are separate
- Offline behavior is expected and graceful
- Physical indicators must match software state
- Every adapter has timeouts, health state, and disable controls
- Administrator privileges are never assumed
