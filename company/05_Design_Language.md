# Design Language

## Experience

Cipher should feel alive but not distracting: dark architectural space, deliberate light, quiet motion, strong hierarchy, and clear state indicators.

## Foundations

- Deep black and navy surfaces
- Cyan as system intelligence, adjusted by identity accents
- Violet for reflection and creative processing
- Warm amber/red only for attention and risk
- White for decisions and primary content
- Negative space as a premium material

## Motion

- Breathing establishes presence.
- Blinks should be irregular and natural.
- Eye motion should be subtle and bounded.
- Speaking uses waveform rhythm.
- Thinking uses rotational analysis rings.
- Listening expands outward.
- Alerts attract attention without panic.
- Reduced-motion preferences must be respected.

## Interaction

Controls must state whether they are real, simulated, disabled, or waiting for approval. Primary actions should be obvious; destructive actions should be visually secondary and confirmed.

## Accessibility

Color is never the only status signal. Text remains readable against cinematic backgrounds. Controls are keyboard accessible, focus-visible, and responsive without horizontal overflow.
