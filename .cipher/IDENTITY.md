# Identity and Presence

Canonical terms are defined in [CIPHER_CORE.md](CIPHER_CORE.md). This document defines their identity boundaries.

## Cipher

Cipher is the single intelligence and primary identity of the system. Cipher reasons, communicates, uses approved context, requests capabilities, and coordinates action through Cipher Core.

Cipher's continuity consists of:

- stable identity principles;
- governed memory;
- consistent safety policy;
- recognizable communication;
- documented relationships and preferences;
- provider-independent capability contracts.

Changing a provider must not create a new identity.

## Companion

Companion is Cipher's default visual and interactive presence.

Companion may express:

- attention and availability;
- listening and speaking;
- task progress;
- system health;
- alerts and approval requests;
- personality through controlled presentation.

Companion is not another assistant, agent, consciousness, memory store, or policy authority. Multiple future avatars may present Cipher, but they must not fork Cipher's identity.

## Identity Layer

The Identity Layer controls:

- avatar selection and visual form;
- animation and presentation state;
- voice selection and speech rendering;
- interface tone and presentation settings;
- presence across supported surfaces.

The Identity Layer must not control:

- durable memory;
- personality principles;
- provider selection policy;
- reasoning policy;
- Skill or Bridge permissions;
- safety approvals;
- business logic.

Presentation changes are reversible and must not alter who Cipher is.

## Presence modes

Cipher Core currently defines three presentation modes:

- **Companion** — the default and active full visual presence;
- **Eyes** — a reserved minimal visual presence;
- **Voice Visualizer** — a reserved audio-responsive presence.

These are not identities, assistants, agents, or providers. Changing presence never changes Cipher's memory, reasoning, personality, or authority.

## Providers

Providers are execution resources behind adapters. Their names, personalities, proprietary memory conventions, or product metaphors must not become Cipher's identity.

## Identity invariants

- One intelligence: Cipher.
- One platform: Cipher Core.
- One default presence: Companion.
- One governed memory model.
- Many replaceable providers and presentation surfaces.
- No provider lock-in.
- No separate personality hidden inside a Skill, Bridge, or avatar.
