# Business Memory

Stores durable business context required for correct product and operational decisions.

## Appropriate content

- product principles;
- customer and market constraints;
- operating policies;
- compliance obligations;
- approved business terminology;
- stable ownership and escalation paths.

## Rules

- Distinguish policy from preference.
- Date regulations and compliance facts.
- Verify high-impact business assumptions before implementation.
- Do not infer authorization from business context.

## Entries

No business memory entries are defined by this operating-manual revision.
